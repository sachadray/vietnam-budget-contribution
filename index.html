    How this was put together

    Sources

    CafeF labels each list by its publication year, not the year of the payments. The lists it labels 2026 measure amounts remitted in 2025, and the VNTAX 200 it labels 2025 measures 2024. Every year shown on this page is the payment year. PetroVietnam is ₫90,991 bn in both 2026-labelled lists, which is how the convention was confirmed.

    VNTAX 200  STATE 100  PRIVATE 100

    Years and currency

    A year means a financial year, running January to December. Vietnam’s financial year is the calendar year, so “2025” on this page is 1 January to 31 December 2025. Years are written plainly as 2024 and 2025, without an FY prefix.

    Every figure is financial year 2025 unless the heading, axis or column says 2024. A year on this page always means the year the money was remitted, never the year the source list was published.

    Dollar figures convert at period-average rates of for 2024 and for 2025. These rates are placeholders pending an official series and are set in one constant in the page data. Every share, percentage and decomposition on this page is computed in dong and is unaffected by the rate.

    Classifying ownership

    State-owned means the state holds control, central or provincial, including state-controlled joint ventures. Firms appearing on neither the STATE 100 nor the PRIVATE 100 were classified by hand; each override is documented in the accompanying workbook.

    Calls worth knowing about

    • Vietsovpetro counted as state: PetroVietnam holds 51%.
    • Sabeco counted as foreign: ThaiBev holds 53.6% while the ministry retains 36%.
    • Nghi Son Refinery counted as foreign: Idemitsu and KPI hold 70.2% against PetroVietnam’s 25.1%.

    What this is not

    • Not a census. Both lists are cut-offs, the STATE 100 at ₫468 bn.
    • Not tax incidence. Remittance bundles taxes the firm bears with value added tax and withheld income tax it collects on the state’s behalf.
    • Not audited accounts. These are publisher figures, reconciled against the publisher’s own published totals but not against Ministry of Finance returns.
